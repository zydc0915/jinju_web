POSITIONING PRINCIPLE

Turn range and angle into coordinates.

The station measures elevation, azimuth and range relative to the tag. The positioning engine combines these observations with installation geometry to calculate coordinates for the business platform.

Selection notes · The brochure identifies Shenzhen Pengyue Technology Co., Ltd. as the manufacturer; Jinju provides product selection enquiries on this site. Its overview states 30cm–1m, while the functional table states 0.5–1m unobstructed; this page uses the latter. 4G, Wi-Fi, speaker and alarm functions depend on model or order. SELECTION QUESTIONS

Selecting a UWB AoA anchor

How does UWB angle of arrival positioning work on PY-U360?

The product measures azimuth, elevation and range and forwards observations to a server-side positioning engine. Confirm station placement, coordinate calibration, compatible tags and the engine interface together when evaluating the UWB AoA anchor.

Is single-anchor UWB positioning suitable for every area?

The brochure describes single-station 1D and 2D positioning. That does not establish unrestricted 3D coverage or positioning through every obstruction. Validate the intended area and target height; the functional table states 0.5–1 m under unobstructed conditions. AoA support does not establish TDoA or PDoA firmware compatibility.
