HOW IT WORKS

## From radio signals to usable location.

Dual-channel UWB stations and directional antennas observe corridor position, then map it to chainage and zones. The specification describes single-station 1D positioning; confirm ranging sequences and direction detection with compatible tags.

DEPLOYMENT ESSENTIALS

## Check the deployment requirements.

01 JJ-U nominal ranging accuracy is 30cm and the 400m transmission figure assumes line of sight. Bends and vehicles may require additional coverage.

02 Single-station 1D support is not unrestricted 2D/3D positioning. Branches need suitable maps and deployment planning.

03 Match tag channels and protocols. Select equipment for hazardous areas using its actual certificates and project requirements.
