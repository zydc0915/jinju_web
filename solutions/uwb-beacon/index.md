## From radio signals to usable location.

UWB beacon describes a broadcast or reference-node role, not one specific algorithm. Systems may use two-way ranging (TWR), uplink TDoA or downlink reference broadcasts. Establish signal direction and synchronization requirements first.

## UWB indoor positioning and asset tracking

### What does a UWB RTLS need?

Ultra-wideband positioning requires compatible tags, anchors and a location engine. For UWB asset tracking, confirm the radio channel, ranging or timestamp mode, firmware and output interfaces together. Bluetooth support on a device does not make all UWB modes available.

[View the Bluetooth UWB asset tag](https://www.rpilink.com/products/product-05/)

### How should warehouse asset tracking be evaluated?

Test the intended tag positions around racks, stock, vehicles and aisle intersections. Specify coordinate or zone requirements, update intervals and track continuity. Recheck coverage with representative inventory and traffic before accepting a deployment.
