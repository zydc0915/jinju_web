HOW IT WORKS

From radio signals to usable location.

UWB beacon describes a broadcast or reference-node role, not one specific algorithm. Systems may use two-way ranging (TWR), uplink TDoA or downlink reference broadcasts. Establish signal direction and synchronization requirements first.
