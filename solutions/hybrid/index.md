SELECTION QUESTIONS

## Designing a personnel tracking system across buildings

### What changes between indoor personnel tracking and outdoor tracking?

A worker tracking system may use Bluetooth, AoA or UWB indoors and supported satellite positioning outdoors. Keep a consistent worker-to-device identity and track the source, age and quality of each location. Source switching and alerts require platform integration and validation.

[Compare personnel tracking badge models](https://www.rpilink.com/products/product-18/)

### LoRa vs LoRaWAN: are the devices interchangeable?

LoRa radio support does not by itself establish LoRaWAN compatibility. Confirm protocol, regional frequency plan and gateway support. The communication link transports observations or coordinates; it does not by itself provide the indoor positioning accuracy.
