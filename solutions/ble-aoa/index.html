HOW IT WORKS

From radio signals to usable location.

Compatible tags transmit direction-finding signals. A receiving antenna array collects phase information to estimate the angle of arrival. The engine combines gateway coordinates, orientation and target height or multiple observations to calculate position.

DEPLOYMENT ESSENTIALS

Check the deployment requirements.

01Indoor JJ-IS40BXPA accuracy is specified as <0.5m unobstructed; the outdoor version specifies within 1m. Do not interchange these figures.

02A coverage radius of about 1.5 times mounting height is nominal. Account for blind spots, target height and edge accuracy.

03Standard iBeacon devices or a Bluetooth 5.x label alone do not establish AoA support. Verify direction-finding packets and firmware.

SELECTION QUESTIONS

BLE AoA positioning for equipment and personnel

How does Bluetooth direction finding support BLE AoA asset tracking?

A compatible tag sends signals to an antenna-array gateway, which estimates their arrival angle. The location engine uses station coordinates and installation geometry to calculate position. Gateway orientation, mounting height and obstructions need to be included in calibration.

View the indoor BLE AoA gateway

What should a hospital asset tracking trial measure?

For medical equipment, test room transitions, detection around beds and metal carts, tag attachment and battery policy. Agree whether the goal is room identification or coordinates, then validate the chosen gateway and tag combination in representative occupied spaces.
