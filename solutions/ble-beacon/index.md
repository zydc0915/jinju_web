HOW IT WORKS

## From radio signals to usable location.

Beacons periodically broadcast an identifier. Fixed gateways can collect moving-tag broadcasts, or mobile terminals can scan fixed beacons. The platform estimates location using RSSI, zone rules or fingerprints. Choose the direction of deployment during system design.

SELECTION QUESTIONS

## Bluetooth indoor positioning: selecting a beacon system

### When is BLE indoor positioning suitable?

Bluetooth beacon tracking suits room or zone detection when a gateway can associate received broadcasts with a location area. RSSI-based estimates depend on walls, shelving, people and calibration; BLE asset tracking should be tested against the required zone boundaries.

[Compare Bluetooth beacon variants](https://www.rpilink.com/products/product-03/)

### Do Bluetooth beacons provide AoA positioning?

A generic beacon broadcast does not establish Bluetooth direction-finding compatibility. AoA needs a compatible transmitting tag, antenna-array receiver and positioning engine. Confirm the signal format and firmware before replacing an RSSI system.

[Review the AoA hardware requirements](https://www.rpilink.com/solutions/ble-aoa/)
